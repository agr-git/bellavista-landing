Three weeks in on the anaerobic run. We've been holding the sealed tanks in the beneficio at 16°C, buffered by the cold nights we get at 1,420 metres.

The goal isn't aggressive — we're after something clean and floral, not a funky science experiment. Pink Bourbon already leans jasmine-and-bergamot in the cup; the fermentation is there to deepen the structure, not rewrite it.

Midweek pH came in at 3.9. We'll keep this batch going another four days, then wash and dry on raised beds. First cupping scheduled for late May.
